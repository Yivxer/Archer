## Design Context

### Users
Archer web is for early invited users who want to try a personal AI that can understand their context before deeper terminal or desktop use. Most first touches happen on mobile, often inside a mobile browser or WeChat, so the page must feel calm, readable, and resilient during typing.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, perceptive, and steadily growing. Archer should feel like a serious personal tool with ritual warmth, not a generic SaaS landing page and not a mystical performance.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current direction is refined, atmospheric, and organic: a restrained dark/light shell, a recognizable gradient ring, Chinese serif/WenKai warmth, sparse copy, and soft motion around state changes. Keep the palette broad enough to avoid one-note blue-purple AI styling; use cyan, blue, amber, orange, and red as brand spectrum accents with tinted neutrals.

### Design Principles
- Make the actual Archer experience the first screen, not a marketing detour.
- Prioritize mobile input, safe areas, and keyboard behavior over decorative complexity.
- Keep copy short and human; errors should sound like a gentle correction, not a system failure.
- Use motion to explain transitions between invitation, Soul building, and chat; avoid constant visual noise.
- Preserve privacy and local-first expectations: web entry can guide and register, but should not imply conversation data is stored in the cloud.
